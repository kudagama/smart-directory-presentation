Directory

01)Problem / Opportunity
 
The information displayed  changes based on the characters entered, which can sometimes  make it a hassle to find the right result.
Problems Identified

❌ Search results continuously change while users type

❌ Similar names generate multiple results

❌ Difficult to identify the correct contact quickly

❌ Time wasted searching for departments and responsible personnel

❌ Increased call handling time for Contact Centre staff

Why It Matters
Delays customer issue resolution
Reduces employee productivity
Increases Average Handling Time (AHT)
Causes frustration when urgent contacts are needed

Opportunity: Use AI to transform the traditional directory into an intelligent search assistant.


02)Proposed Solution / Innovation

Key Innovations

✅ Natural Language Search

Search using simple everyday language.

✅ Smart Result Identification

AI finds and prioritizes the most relevant contact.

✅ Single Search Experience

Search people, departments, locations, and organizations from one place.

✅ Conversational Interface

Users ask questions instead of using complex filters.

✅ Faster Contact Discovery

Reduces time spent searching and improves productivity.

     Operational Efficiency

    Customer Experience
