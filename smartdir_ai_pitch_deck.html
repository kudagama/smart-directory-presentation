The Problem & Opportunity

Why modernizing the SLT contact center is crucial right now.

High Average Handling Time

Agents spend valuable time manually typing and searching directories while the customer is kept waiting on hold.

Agent Burnout

Continuous manual data entry and searching leads to cognitive fatigue, impacting the quality of service provided.

Lack of Real-time QA

Call auditing happens days later. Missing information or incorrect advice isn't caught in real-time.

The Human Touch

Sri Lankan customers prefer speaking to human agents. Standard IVR bots cause frustration and drop-offs.

Proposed Solution

The Agent Copilot: Empathy from humans, speed from AI.

Real-Time Listening

AI securely streams audio during the call, transcribing mixed Sinhala/English (Singlish) in milliseconds.

Intent Extraction

An LLM extracts exact requirements (e.g., location, organization name) without manual input.

Zero-Click Lookup

Results instantly pop up on the agent's screen. The agent simply reads it to the customer naturally.

Post-Call Analytics & Wellness

AI continues to work even after the customer hangs up.

Smart Quality Assurance

  • Instant Summary: Generates a brief text summary of the call immediately after termination.

  • Error Detection: Highlights if the agent provided incorrect info or missed mandatory greetings.

  • 100% Coverage: Analyzes every single call instead of random manual sampling.

Agent Fatigue Detection

Beyond technical metrics, the AI monitors the human element. By analyzing speech patterns, voice pitch, and response latency over a shift, it detects signs of cognitive fatigue.

Agent Wellness Score Needs Break

Alert triggered to Team Lead to arrange a rotation.
