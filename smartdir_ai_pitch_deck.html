Section 01

Problem / Opportunity

⚠️

The Core Challenge: "The information displayed changes based on the characters entered, which can sometimes make it a hassle to find the right result."

Problems Identified

Search results continuously change while users type

Shifting characters constantly alter filtered results, forcing backspacing.

Similar names generate multiple results

Dozens of identical or near-identical names without clear hierarchy.

Difficult to identify the correct contact quickly

Lacks intelligent contextual sorting or distinction by active designation.

Time wasted searching for departments & responsible personnel

Users spend vital minutes manually navigating complex organizational charts.

Increased call handling time for Contact Centre staff

Agents spend crucial on-call seconds searching while customers wait on hold.

Why It Matters

Delays customer issue resolution
Reduces employee productivity
Increases Average Handling Time (AHT)
Causes frustration when urgent contacts are needed
The Opportunity

Use AI to transform the traditional directory into an intelligent search assistant.

Faster Contact Discovery

Contact Centre

Agent Copilot Integration

Seamless integration with 1912 hotline agent screens for zero-click contact popups.

Post-Call Analytics & Wellness

AI continues to work even after the customer hangs up.

Smart Quality Assurance

  • Instant Summary: Generates a brief text summary of the call immediately after termination.

  • Error Detection: Highlights if the agent provided incorrect info or missed mandatory greetings.

  • 100% Coverage: Analyzes every single call instead of random manual sampling.

Agent Fatigue Detection

Beyond technical metrics, the AI monitors the human element. By analyzing speech patterns, voice pitch, and response latency over a shift, it detects signs of cognitive fatigue.

Agent Wellness Score Needs Break

Alert triggered to Team Lead to arrange a rotation.
